Privacy policy

App: Galactic Log, for DoorDash Dashers

Developer: Galactic Log

Contact: dashlog.support@gmail.com

Effective: October 9, 2026

Galactic Log helps delivery drivers track their earnings, miles and expenses. It isn't made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by DoorDash.

The short version

Everything the app records stays on your phone. The app has no internet access: it can't send your data anywhere, and we (the developer) never receive it unless you email us a problem report. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. Your data only leaves your phone when you choose to back it up or export it, to a place you pick.

What leaves your phone

Only what you send yourself:

  • Backups, to a folder or file you choose (on the phone, or a cloud drive you pick in Android's file picker).
  • Exports (mileage log CSV, tax summary PDF, earnings CSV), saved or shared where you choose.
  • Problem reports, if you choose to send one ("Report a problem", or after the app closes unexpectedly). You write what happened and tick what to include: crash logs (the error, the app version and your phone model), the app's status (which permissions are on and whether capture, GPS and backups are working), and screen samples (the text of DoorDash's screens from the last 7 days, with pay, stores and times, and customers' names and addresses already removed). The report opens in your email app as an attachment; nothing is sent until you send it there. We use reports only to fix the app, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

We never see anything else. No data is sold or shared with anyone, including DoorDash.

Crash logs are kept on your phone (the last 5) until you send or delete them; clearing the app's storage deletes them.

Permissions

  • Location ("while using the app", and optionally "all the time"): mileage tracking, described above.
  • Notifications: offer alerts, dash reminders, odometer prompts and pause warnings.
  • Notification access (optional): DoorDash's notifications, described above.
  • Accessibility service (optional): the screen capture, described above.
  • Run at startup: to carry on tracking a dash that was open when the phone restarted.

Keeping and deleting your data

To ask about your data, or to have a problem report you sent deleted, email dashlog.support@gmail.com.

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete any order, dash or expense in the app, delete all screen samples from the Capture screen, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Backups you made stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

The app is for delivery drivers, who must be adults. It isn't meant for children.
